About Me

I'm a Computer Engineering graduate and Data Analyst & Data Scientist focused on using data to uncover insights, solve problems, and support better decision-making.

My work spans the data lifecycle — from data cleaning and exploratory analysis to visualization, statistical analysis, machine learning, and communicating results through clear and meaningful insights.

I work primarily with Python, SQL, Power BI, Excel, and machine learning tools to transform raw data into practical solutions. This portfolio showcases projects across analytics, business intelligence, predictive modeling, and data science.
